## Record loading redesigned

Searching every chat was slow because reading a cached Chat.Turn cost about
thirty times its JSON parse: every row was validated twice, with copies and a
"nothing was transformed" comparison. The User traced that comparison to an
unrequested runtime version of a round-trip test asked for in September, and
redesigned loading: records are parsed once by pydantic into typed data, a
standard pipeline hands builders a `LoadedRecord`, check levels stay in the
loading phase, and no object keeps the record it came from. See
[Loading a Record](../record-interfaces.md#loading-a-record--921) and requests
921 and 922.

A side quest found why chatlas-context audits were slow: they rebuilt every
cited session's Normal Model from raw. They now read cached Turns, fresh or
stale, and rebuild only to confirm a Turn the cache does not hold; the
ARCHITECTURE.md audit went from 34 s to 2 s.
